Use real Org headings and one monotonically increasing project-wide work number.
Never reuse an identifier, including after abandonment or archival. A work item
heading has the form "** TODO WNNN - Concise descriptor :type:" and properties
for CUSTOM_ID, TYPE, PRIORITY, SOURCE, and CREATED. Put acceptance criteria,
test definitions, edge cases, and solution design in the linked spec.org.
