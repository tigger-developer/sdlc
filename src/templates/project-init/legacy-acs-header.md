# LEGACY DOCUMENT

<!-- SDLC-MANAGED-BLOCK: LEGACY-ACS -->

This document is the authoritative record of requirements established under the
legacy ticket-led process, including current and superseded requirements,
provenance, and test traceability. Requirements established or changed through
Spec Kit are governed by approved `specs/*/spec.md` artefacts.

The migration state and the disposition of non-delivered legacy scope are
recorded in `docs/ticket-migration.org`. After that record marks migration
complete, no issue body, comment, or archived ticket remains a current
requirement authority outside this document.
