* Synopsis
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:

** Outcome

- The SDLC *distinguishes ticket-scoped automation* from the *persistent regression pack* and gives repeatable operations *fourfold execution headroom* without increasing monetary authority.

** Before

- Automated checks outside =make test= are classified as one-off tests and do not follow TDD.
- Agent-authored specifications can set a proof allowance so narrow that diagnosis or a corrected rerun requires renewed authority.

** After

- *AT and RT follow RED/GREEN TDD*; only RT belongs to the persistent regression pack.
- *OT means genuinely one-off* and *UT means human-dependent*; user-experience changes require eventual UT sign-off.
- Safe repeatable operational limits are *at least four times the expected usage*, while monetary budgets are *never multiplied*.

** Changes

- Standards, templates, workflow skills, audit prompts, help, public documentation and readiness tooling *use the four test categories consistently*.

** Unchanged

- Existing AC, RT, OT and UT identifiers remain valid, historical artefacts remain unchanged, and =make test= remains the persistent regression entry point.

** Edge cases

- Genuinely one-off, irreversible and hard externally constrained operations *do not receive artificial fourfold authority*.
- Existing specifications without AT records *retain their current validity and readiness meaning*.

** Decisions

- AT and RT are sibling categories with one TDD contract; retention in =make test= is the distinguishing property.
- Definition approval and delivery confirmation authorize one exact monetary budget; covered calls do not require per-call approval.

** Next step

- Definition audit, then operator sign-off before implementation.

* Context
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: context
:END:

** Problem

The current three-category model places automated, repeatable, ticket-scoped
checks under OT. =TESTING.md= then says OTs do not follow TDD, while the word
"one-off" can be read as permitting only one execution attempt. Specifications
have consequently used exact proof-call counts as complete execution ceilings,
leaving no allowance for diagnosis, transient failure or corrected reruns.

** Outcome

The framework defines four unambiguous test categories and requires realistic
operational headroom whenever an agent writes bounded repeatable execution into
a specification.

** Scope

*** In scope

- Normative test classification, TDD, readiness and evidence rules.
- Specification and validation templates, including the next template schema.
- Normal, paired and emergency workflow skills and audit prompts.
- Deterministic Org parsing, readiness inventory and stage eligibility.
- Current command help, active public documentation, changelog and durable design learnings.

*** Out of scope

- Rewriting existing specifications, validation records, archived documentation or legacy migration evidence.
- Adding AT records retrospectively to delivered work.
- Changing =make test= to execute ticket-scoped ATs.
- Increasing a monetary budget, irreversible action count or hard external limit.
- Changing audit-provider retry or verdict budgets.

*** Unchanged

- Existing RT, OT and UT identifiers and evidence remain valid.
- =make test= remains the stable complete persistent regression command.
- Metered operations remain excluded from regression, CI, build and scheduled automation.
- Operator approval remains required for spending, irreversible outcomes, external-state changes and final closure.

** Constraints and dependencies
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: constraints-and-dependencies
:END:

- The change must remain backward-compatible with every valid existing v3 specification and validation record.
- The new AT category must be machine-checkable before active workflow text can require it.
- The template schema must increment once because the authoring contract changes.
- The public release must follow the repository's semantic-versioned release discipline.

** Decisions

- AT means an automated ticket test: agent-executable, governed by TDD and excluded from the persistent regression pack.
- RT means an automated regression test: governed by the same TDD contract and retained in =make test=.
- OT is reserved for genuinely non-repeatable verification or one authorized live event; repeatable automation outside =make test= is AT.
- UT requires human participation or judgement. Every user-experience change includes eventual UT sign-off.
- The fourfold rule applies to execution counts for safe repeatable operations, not to money or genuinely one-off authority.
- Existing AT implementation may be removed after delivery or retained behind an explicit manual diagnostic entry point, but it must not enter regression, CI, build or scheduled execution.

** Unresolved blockers

- None.

* Acceptance Criteria
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: acceptance-criteria
:END:

** AC023.1 - Separate ticket automation from persistent regression :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-1
:END:

- Criterion :: Given an automated check for new or changed behaviour, the SDLC classifies it as AT when it is ticket-scoped and excluded from =make test=, or RT when it remains in the persistent regression pack; both categories require an intended RED result before implementation and GREEN afterwards, except for a justified initially GREEN preservation test.
- Proven by :: [[#rt023-1][RT023.1 - Recognize all four test categories]] and [[#rt023-2][RT023.2 - Enforce automated-test readiness]].

** AC023.2 - Reserve OT and UT for their actual boundaries :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-2
:END:

- Criterion :: Given verification outside AT or RT, OT is used only for a genuinely non-repeatable operation or one authorized live event whose specification explains why repeatable automation is unsuitable, while UT is used whenever completion requires human participation or judgement; every user-experience change defines an eventual UT.
- Proven by :: [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

** AC023.3 - Give repeatable operations fourfold headroom :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-3
:END:

- Criterion :: Given an agent-authored specification that bounds API calls, commands, rebuilds, inspections, probes, retries or another safe repeatable operation, it records expected executions and an authorized maximum of at least four times that estimate, allocated independently across proof, diagnosis, corrected rerun and contingency for each boundary.
- Proven by :: [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

** AC023.4 - Keep time, safety and monetary limits distinct :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-4
:END:

- Criterion :: Given an increased execution allowance, the per-attempt timeout remains based on one attempt and the aggregate time accommodates the authorized maximum; the multiplier does not increase monetary budgets, genuinely one-off operations, irreversible actions or hard external constraints.
- Proven by :: [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

** AC023.5 - Authorize one exact monetary budget at two stages :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-5
:END:

- Criterion :: Given any monetary exposure, the specification records the exact budget for definition approval and the operator confirms it once before delivery begins; covered expenditure within that confirmed total requires no per-call authorization and the amount is not multiplied by the operational allowance rule.
- Proven by :: [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

** AC023.6 - Enforce AT without invalidating existing records :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-6
:END:

- Criterion :: Given a valid specification and validation record, the deterministic tooling recognizes and inventories AT alongside RT, OT and UT; test-code requires AT and RT to be RED or GREEN, delivery-code requires AT, RT and OT to be GREEN, UT may remain AMBER, and records containing only the existing categories retain their former result.
- Proven by :: [[#rt023-1][RT023.1 - Recognize all four test categories]] and [[#rt023-2][RT023.2 - Enforce automated-test readiness]].

** AC023.7 - Keep every active workflow surface consistent :ac:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ac023-7
:END:

- Criterion :: Given normal, paired or emergency delivery, active standards, templates, skills, audit prompts, command help and public documentation use the same four categories, apply test-code whenever AT or RT exists, require current AT/RT/OT GREEN before delivery-code, and leave historical or archived evidence unchanged.
- Proven by :: [[#rt023-3][RT023.3 - Preserve workflow and installation behaviour]] and [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

* Edge Cases
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: edge-cases
:END:

** Initially GREEN preservation automation

- Condition :: Existing behaviour is protected by a new AT or RT that already passes.
- Required behaviour :: Record its preservation purpose rather than manufacturing RED.
- Covered by :: [[#ac023-1][AC023.1 - Separate ticket automation from persistent regression]].

** Cost-free API with bounded calls

- Condition :: A repeatable API call has no monetary charge but still has operational or rate-limit bounds.
- Required behaviour :: Apply the fourfold execution allowance unless a hard external limit prevents it; do not invent a monetary budget.
- Covered by :: [[#ac023-3][AC023.3 - Give repeatable operations fourfold headroom]] and [[#ac023-4][AC023.4 - Keep time, safety and monetary limits distinct]].

** Destructive verification

- Condition :: An operation cannot safely be repeated or rolled back.
- Required behaviour :: Classify its verification as OT, authorize the exact operation, define partial-failure recovery and exclude it from the fourfold multiplier; pair execution when operator participation is required, without reclassifying objective evidence as UT.
- Covered by :: [[#ac023-2][AC023.2 - Reserve OT and UT for their actual boundaries]] and [[#ac023-4][AC023.4 - Keep time, safety and monetary limits distinct]].

** Existing specifications and evidence

- Condition :: A project created before this release contains only RT, OT and UT records.
- Required behaviour :: The validator reports the same validity, readiness and evidence requirements as before; no migration or renumbering is required.
- Covered by :: [[#ac023-6][AC023.6 - Enforce AT without invalidating existing records]].

* Solution Design
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: solution-design
:END:

** Architecture context

- Relevant components :: Normative Markdown standards under =src/=, active Org templates, global workflow skills, composite audit prompts, Go readiness parsing and CLI help, installer fixtures and public onboarding documentation.
- Ownership boundary :: =TESTING.md= owns classification, TDD and execution allowances; =ORG-SCHEMA.md= owns record and readiness semantics; templates and skills apply those rules without becoming competing authorities.
- Existing pattern :: Test IDs use a two-letter prefix and the validator derives category counts and stage rules from that prefix.

** High-level design

*** Components and responsibilities

- =TESTING.md= defines AT, RT, OT and UT, the shared AT/RT TDD lifecycle, execution allowances and monetary authorization.
- =ORG-SCHEMA.md= admits AT identifiers and specifies their evidence and readiness states.
- The v3 specification template advances from schema 8 to schema 9, adds AT definitions and fields for expected executions, authorized maximum, allocation, timeouts, total time, stop condition and exact monetary budget where bounded operations apply.
- The validation template gains an AT section with command and run history while retaining the RT, OT and UT sections.
- Definition and delivery skills, paired/emergency workflows and audit prompts select and enforce the same categories and gate sequencing.
- The Go readiness package adds an =at= inventory count, accepts AT IDs and applies RT-equivalent command and execution requirements to AT.
- Current help, overview, quickstart, architecture, changelog and learning records describe the released model; historical specifications and archived documentation remain evidence of their own releases.

*** Reuse, shared behaviour and variation

- Shared operation :: One automated-test predicate covers AT and RT for command evidence and test-code readiness.
- Variation :: Only RT is retained under =make test=; AT remains ticket-scoped. OT and UT keep separate evidence and timing rules.

*** Boundaries and dependencies

- No new dependency, service, configuration key, provider integration or test framework is introduced.
- The YAML inventory adds =at= without removing or changing existing keys.
- Existing identifiers need no migration because AT is additive and newly allocated only.

*** Flow

1. Definition selects AT, RT, OT and UT honestly and records expected and maximum bounded executions.
2. AT and RT are written and executed before production implementation, then reviewed at test-code.
3. Implementation obtains current GREEN AT, RT and OT evidence; UT may await the operator until closure.
4. After delivery, AT remains outside =make test= while RT continues protecting regression behaviour.

*** Essential mechanisms

- Add =AT= to the Org identifier allowlist, category inventory and execution-evidence predicate.
- Replace RT-only test-code conditions with an AT-or-RT automated predicate.
- Replace no-RT gate skipping with no-AT-and-no-RT gate skipping.
- Preserve the delivery-code rule that every non-UT test must be GREEN.
- Keep the fourfold rule as an authoring and audit obligation; do not build budget-management or speculative numeric inference into the validator.

*** Interfaces and configuration

- Test identifier :: =ATNNN.N= and =AT-NNN.N= follow the existing test identifier grammar.
- Inventory output :: =audit-readiness.inventory.counts.at= reports the AT count; existing count keys retain their meanings.
- Stable command :: =make test= continues to run RTs only. ATs use an explicit ticket-defined command and never enter automatic persistent targets.
- Compatibility :: Existing RT/OT/UT records and command invocations remain valid without conversion.

*** Data and state

- Specifications and validation records may contain new AT headings after this release.
- Existing durable histories remain untouched. Superseded AT executions follow the same subordinate run-history contract as other tests.

*** Failure behaviour

- Unknown prefixes remain schema errors. AT missing command evidence or left AMBER blocks test-code; any non-GREEN AT blocks delivery-code.
- A specification below fourfold headroom without an applicable exception fails definition review rather than receiving an invented value from tooling.
- Exhausted operational or monetary authority stops execution; the agent cannot silently expand either ceiling.

*** Simplicity and proportionality

- One new category and one shared automated predicate express the distinction directly. No hierarchy, plugin, budget engine or new gate is introduced.

** Design decisions and verification

*** Use sibling automated categories

- Decision :: AT and RT are sibling identifiers governed by one TDD contract.
- Rationale :: Treating AT as a superclass would make individual records ambiguous and complicate inventory and readiness without adding useful behaviour.
- Verification :: [[#rt023-1][RT023.1 - Recognize all four test categories]] and [[#rt023-2][RT023.2 - Enforce automated-test readiness]].

*** Keep allowance policy declarative

- Decision :: Definition and audit enforce expected-versus-maximum execution fields; the validator does not calculate or authorize operational limits.
- Rationale :: Arithmetic alone cannot decide whether an operation is safe, genuinely one-off, irreversible or externally constrained.
- Verification :: [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

*** Preserve historical records

- Decision :: Update active sources and generated interfaces only; do not rewrite archives, prior specifications or validation evidence.
- Verification :: [[#rt023-1][RT023.1 - Recognize all four test categories]] and [[#ot023-1][OT023.1 - Review the active classification contract]].

** Routine details left to implementation

- Exact helper names and test fixture composition within the existing readiness package.
- Line wrapping and cross-reference placement that preserve the approved wording and document standards.

** Architecture impact

*** Security impact

- Threat boundary :: No trust boundary changes. The fourfold allowance applies only to operations already authorized and safe to repeat.
- Data and secrets :: No new data, credential access, logging or retention is introduced.
- Access :: No access widens. Irreversible, destructive, hard-limited and monetary operations retain explicit exact authority.
- Conclusion :: The principal risk is accidental authority expansion; explicit exclusions, per-boundary limits and unchanged monetary ceilings constrain it.

*** Other relevant qualities

- Compatibility :: Additive AT parsing and unchanged existing keys avoid a migration requirement.
- Operability :: Fourfold per-boundary headroom reduces avoidable operator interruption while retaining stop conditions and hard ceilings.
- Maintainability :: One canonical classification and allowance rule prevents skills and templates from developing conflicting semantics.
